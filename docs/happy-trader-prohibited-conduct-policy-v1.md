# Happy Trader Funding — Trader Pledge & Prohibited Conduct Policy (V1)

> **Product policy — not final legal Terms.** This document is the plain-language
> product policy that the Happy Trader platform enforces. It is written to be fair
> and specific, not to teach circumvention. It is **not** attorney-approved Terms
> of Service and **must be reviewed by counsel before production launch.** Where
> this policy and your signed customer agreement differ, the signed agreement
> governs.

Policy version: **v1**

Our promise, in one line: **we protect customers, payouts, identities, payments
and the platform — we do not punish you for being good at trading.**

---

## What is always allowed

You will **never** be reviewed or penalised *merely* for any of the following:

- Being profitable — including highly profitable, or profitable after earlier losses.
- A high win rate, a large single payout, or reaching your payout caps.
- Scalping, fast trading, short holding periods, or many trades in a day
  (subject only to your own personal risk controls or explicit product rules).
- News trading and trading in volatile markets.
- Holding overnight **while the supported market/session is open.**
- Using stops, or choosing not to use stops.
- Discretionary or systematic trading through supported interfaces.
- Trading a single instrument, or many of the supported instruments
  (NQ/MNQ, ES/MES, GC/MGC, CL/MCL).
- Using a **VPN**, **travelling**, **changing networks**, or using **multiple of
  your own personal devices.**
- Happy Trader **native copy trading across your own eligible accounts** — even
  when that produces identical trades across those accounts.
- Taking trades that happen to be **similar to other customers'** trades.
- Withdrawing every eligible payout and completing all five payout cycles.
- Using your personal risk controls, or disabling *Flexible* personal controls
  where that is permitted.

There are **no secret "too profitable" rules**, no hidden profit caps, no maximum
win rate, no minimum holding time, and no secret payout-denial trading rules
beyond the published product rules.

## Account ownership

Only the **verified account owner** may operate a Happy Trader account.

**Prohibited:** selling, transferring, or renting an account; giving someone else
your credentials to trade it; third-party account management; "pass-for-hire" or
paying another trader to pass or manage your account; impersonating another
customer; or knowingly operating an account under another person's verified
identity.

**Allowed:** your own devices, travel, changing networks, a VPN, several personal
devices, and supported copy trading across your own accounts. A VPN, a new device,
or travel — on their own — are **not** violations.

## Copy trading

**Allowed:** Happy Trader native copy trading across your own eligible accounts,
and legitimate supported copy software between accounts you own and control where
the integration permits it.

**Prohibited:** copying from an account controlled by another person in a way that
amounts to third-party management; letting another person control your accounts;
or using copy tools to get around account-ownership rules.

Similar or identical trades **alone** are never treated as misconduct.

## Automation

Manual trading, Happy Trader native copy trading, and approved/supported
integrations are allowed. **Prohibited:** reverse-engineering private endpoints,
bypassing client/server controls, tampering with requests, unauthorized order
submission, bypassing risk or lifecycle restrictions, or forging account/order
state. Ordinary browser use and fast clicking are not, by themselves, exploitation.

## Platform integrity

**Prohibited (only when intentional and evidenced):** knowingly exploiting stale
or erroneous prices or duplicate-execution bugs; deliberately triggering race
conditions or impossible fills; exploiting a known defect after recognising it;
tampering with or replaying protected requests; forging execution acknowledgements;
or attempting to alter server-authoritative balances, P&L, risk state, payouts,
certificates or entitlements.

An ordinary malfunction — a bug, a disconnect, a provider failure, latency, or an
order behaving unexpectedly — is **not** misconduct, and you will not be penalised
for it.

## Identity

**Prohibited:** falsified or forged identity information/documents, using another
person's verified identity, deliberate impersonation, or materially false
verification information. A possible data mismatch or provider uncertainty leads
to a **review**, not an automatic finding.

## Payments

**Prohibited:** knowingly using stolen payment credentials, deliberate payment
fraud, or deliberate abuse of refunds/chargebacks. A chargeback by itself does
**not** prove fraud — a duplicate charge, a genuine billing dispute, or a merchant
or provider error remains something we will investigate and make right.

## Payouts

**Prohibited:** requesting a payout under another person's identity, falsifying
payout-destination ownership where verification is required, manipulating payout
records, or knowingly trying to receive the same payout more than once.

## Security

**Prohibited:** credential theft, unauthorized account access, attempts to
compromise another customer's account or Happy Trader systems, session/token
misuse, privilege escalation, or owner/admin impersonation.

## Coordinated abuse

You will **not** be penalised for discussing trades, trading the same instrument
or setup, trading the same market event, belonging to the same community, or using
similar strategies. We only escalate where **multiple independent signals** point
to coordinated activity intended to defeat ownership, payment, payout, platform or
account rules. Trade similarity alone is never enough.

## Rule breaches are not misconduct

Some things end or delay an account but are **not** prohibited conduct:

- Breaching your max loss limit (MLL) is an **account/risk failure**, not fraud.
- Not meeting evaluation consistency is an **eligibility delay**, not misconduct.
- Not reaching the Daily progressive payout balance means a payout is **not yet
  eligible**, not that you did anything wrong.
- A personal risk control blocking an order is **your own setting**, not a penalty.

## Reviews and temporary holds

Sometimes we place a **temporary hold** while we review something — for example, a
payout may be held pending review. A temporary hold is **not** a decision that you
did anything wrong, and we will tell you, in customer-safe language, the general
reason category and whether an action is temporary or final. We do not share
internal detection details, thresholds, or other customers' information.

A trading hold, if one is ever applied, will **never stop you from closing or
reducing a position** — you can always flatten, place protective stops/targets,
reduce size, and cancel orders. It only blocks opening or increasing exposure.

## Enforcement

Punitive action (such as confirming a serious violation, or terminating an
account) requires **sufficient evidence and proper authority**, and is separate
from temporary safety holds. Clear, high-confidence safety actions (for example,
signing you out of all sessions if we believe your account was accessed by someone
else) may happen immediately to protect you — that is containment, not a finding of
wrongdoing.

## Appeals

If you receive an eligible **final adverse decision**, you may appeal it. Your
original decision and your appeal are both kept on record; an appeal is a fresh
review, and where staffing allows it is not decided by the same person who made the
original serious decision. Outcomes can be **upheld**, **overturned**, or
**partially remediated**.

## Platform errors

If a platform, payment, or system error on our side harms you, we will make it
right using our normal, auditable tools — for example restoring an account,
replacing an evaluation, refunding a fee, or releasing a payout. We do not create
money outside our ledger.

## Policy updates

We may update this policy. New versions are tracked, and accepting a new version
does not erase your earlier acceptances. Material changes will be surfaced to you.
