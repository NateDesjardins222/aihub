Bundled fonts for deterministic certificate rendering.

HappyTraderSans-Regular.ttf / HappyTraderSans-Bold.ttf are copies of DejaVu Sans
(and HappyTraderSerif-Regular.ttf a copy of DejaVu Serif), renamed for a stable
family reference. DejaVu fonts are distributed under a free license (Bitstream
Vera license + public-domain additions) that permits bundling and redistribution.
See https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html.

These are NON-BRANDED placeholder faces used until final approved typography is
supplied. They are open-licensed; no proprietary font files are included.
